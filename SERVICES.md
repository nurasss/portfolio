## What a monthly QA cycle can cover

- **Release smoke tests:** sign-in, core customer journeys, forms, checkout or booking flows where applicable.
- **API checks:** representative successful/error responses, authorization boundaries, repeat-request behavior and regression risks.
- **Cross-device review:** responsive layout and key flows on agreed browsers/device profiles.
- **Actionable defect report:** severity, reproduction steps, expected vs. actual results, screenshots or logs when available.
- **Retest:** verify agreed fixes within the monthly hour allocation.
- **Release summary:** tested scope, open risks and recommendations; the product owner makes the release decision.

**Delivery:** agree a prioritized test checklist first; then receive a written findings report and concise release-risk summary. Hours are tracked and shared. Work beyond the 8-hour allowance requires separate approval.
